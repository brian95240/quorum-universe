## Licensing FAQ

### Q: Can I use the GPL version for commercial purposes?
**A:** Yes, but you must comply with GPL-3.0 terms, including making your
source code available under GPL-3.0 if you distribute the software.

### Q: What if my revenue changes during the license term?
**A:** You must upgrade to the appropriate tier within 30 days of exceeding
the revenue threshold for your current tier.

### Q: Can I downgrade my license?
**A:** Downgrades take effect at the next renewal period. No refunds for
mid-term downgrades.

### Q: Is there a trial period?
**A:** Yes, we offer a 30-day evaluation period for Professional tier and
above. Contact sales@quorum-universe.io.

### Q: What payment methods are accepted?
**A:** Credit card, wire transfer, ACH, and purchase orders (Enterprise only).
